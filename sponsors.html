Dear Potential Sponsor,

Thank you for taking the time to learn about Boston Ni Baaje. Founded in 2026 by Maya Mehta and Diya Chatwani, Boston Ni Baaje is Boston’s first official national-level intercollegiate Garba-Raas competition. For us, dance is not just an activity, it is a passion, a community, and a family. It serves as an outlet for expression, identity, and connection, especially for students who have found belonging through Garba-Raas. This event is rooted in that experience and aims to bring it to a larger stage and spread the community in the greater Boston area.

This competition was born from the success and heart of Northeastern Nakhraas, one of the nation’s most recognized collegiate Raas teams. The team’s consistent top-three ranking in the national circuit have elevated Boston’s presence in collegiate dance, creating a platform to celebrate, strengthen, and expand the city’s South Asian dance community. The team believes it is time for Boston to establish its own national Garba-Raas competition, one that honors heritage, uplifts student performers, and builds a lasting cultural tradition in the city.

Boston Ni Baaje will host 6+ competitive teams (200+ students) from universities across the country at the Huntington Theatre, with an anticipated 400+ live audience members and a nationwide livestream.

Boston Ni Baaje is currently working towards registering as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, a percentage of proceeds from the event will go toward the American Heart Association. This event is also an opportunity to uplift businesses that value diversity, culture, and community engagement. Our sponsors play an essential role in helping us bring this vision to life while gaining meaningful exposure to a vibrant audience of students, families, and professionals nationwide.

Boston Ni Baaje offers flexible sponsorship opportunities, including direct donations or product and service collaborations. We are happy to work with you in whichever capacity your business is able to support us. BNB understands that every organization has different capacities, and we deeply value any form of involvement. In supporting Boston Ni Baaje, you are joining a network rooted in culture, passion, and dance, and helping strengthen the community that makes this event possible. Beyond the competition, your partnership also contributes to the American Heart Association, ensuring that the success of Boston Ni Baaje creates meaningful impact both on and off the stage.

This vision is only possible through partners like you. Thank you for your consideration and we look forward to building a meaningful and lasting partnership together.

Sincerely, Anisha Gandhi & Shreyas Lenkala Boston Ni Baaje Sponsorship Team 2026–2027
