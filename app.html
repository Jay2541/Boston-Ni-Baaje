📱 Take BNB With You

Get the App

Chat with your team, get announcements, schedule and more, right from your home screen.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    You’ll use your phone number + PIN to sign in to the app.

  2. 2

    Add BNB to your home screen

    📱 On iPhone (Safari)

    1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
    3. Tap Add. The BNB icon appears on your home screen.

    🤖 On Android (Chrome)

    1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
    2. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”
    3. Confirm. The BNB icon appears on your home screen.
  3. 3

    Open the BNB app and sign in

    Tap the BNB icon on your home screen and sign in with your phone number and PIN.

    You only do this once. The app stays logged in. Then tap Allow to get notifications.

    First time? Get your team’s join link from your captain or liaison.
