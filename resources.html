Sexual Assault Prevention

Boston Ni Baaje (BNB) is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all participants, staff, and spectators. We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy toward sexual assault, sexual harassment, discrimination, bullying, stalking, retaliation, or any abusive behavior. Every member of the BNB community shares the responsibility of treating others with dignity and promoting a culture grounded in consent and respect, regardless of identity or background.

To support our community, a dedicated Safety & Wellness Team will be available throughout competition weekend to offer confidential support, de-escalate situations, and address concerns. Anyone who experiences or witnesses behavior that violates this policy is strongly encouraged to submit a report using our confidential online form, located at the bottom of this page and on the BNB website, or by speaking directly with an on-site Safety & Wellness Team member. All reports will be handled with discretion, urgency, and care.

BNB reserves the right to investigate reported incidents and respond in accordance with this policy, which may include measures to protect the safety of attendees. Policy violations may result in immediate removal from the event, individual or team disqualification, referral to university or organizational officials, and notification of local law enforcement when warranted. By attending or participating in BNB, all individuals agree to uphold these standards and contribute to a safe, supportive environment for everyone.

Statement of Inclusivity

Boston Ni Baaje is dedicated to creating a welcoming, vibrant, and inclusive community where dancers, volunteers, judges, and spectators from all backgrounds feel valued, respected, and celebrated. We believe our strength lies in our diversity and are committed to fostering an environment free from discrimination, prejudice, and marginalization. At BNB, everyone belongs, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, socioeconomic status or disability. We expect all participants and attendees to uphold these values by leading with empathy and supporting one another throughout competition weekend. Through our shared celebration of culture, dance, and community, we strive to ensure that every individual can show up authentically and experience a safe, supportive, and empowering environment.
